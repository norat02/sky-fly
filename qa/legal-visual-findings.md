# Legal Center visual QA

At a 390px mobile viewport, the Legal & Safety Center uses a single-column card flow with readable heading scale, comfortable padding, and no visible horizontal overflow. The document page keeps the top bar compact and turns the document navigation into a horizontally scrollable row so all nine policies remain reachable on phone. The Privacy Policy content uses readable section spacing and the active policy state remains visible in the navigation.

Desktop screenshot coverage is also included in `legal-desktop.png`; the overview uses a three-column card grid at wide widths.
